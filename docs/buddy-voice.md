# Narra Narry — a friendly learning companion

The app should sound like someone sitting beside the learner, helping them try one small thing. Narry uses “tớ” for itself and “bạn” for the learner; “mình thử… nhé” invites a shared next step. English uses natural, brief invitations such as “Let’s try one sentence” and “What shall we talk about?”

Keep instructions concrete, calm and easy to act on. One helpful next step is better than a motivational slogan. Name the action in buttons: listen to a phrase, start a conversation, review a word. Avoid grading language, guilt about a broken streak, exaggerated praise or claims that a local sample has assessed the learner’s ability.

| Situation | Narry voice |
| --- | --- |
| Choosing practice | “Mình nói chuyện gì hôm nay?” / “What shall we talk about?” |
| Feeling unsure | “Chưa cần hoàn hảo. Mình thử một câu trước nhé.” / “One sentence first. There’s no rush to get it perfect.” |
| Starting a café roleplay | “Mình thử ghé một quán cà phê nhé. Bạn muốn gọi món gì nào?” |
| Trying again | “Gần đúng rồi đó. Mình nhìn lại câu mẫu rồi thử thêm nhé.” |
| Recording issue | “Chưa nghe được bạn rồi. Mình thử ghi âm lại nhé?” |
| Writing | “Kể mình nghe một thói quen nhỏ khiến ngày của bạn vui hơn nhé.” |
| After real activity | Acknowledge the recorded review/minutes or completed step, then offer a small next action. |

Narry supports the learner across Lesson, Practice, Dictionary, Profile, preparation, review, conversation and tutor choice. Expandable hints explain a strategy; English phrase audio gives a model; action buttons open the matching exercise or help panel. Hints remain optional and the main speaking/lesson action stays prominent.

Technical limits, microphone permissions, privacy, demo social data and plan previews retain plain, precise wording. Friendly language must not hide a limitation or suggest an unconnected AI service is evaluating the learner.

## Narry introduces itself

The mascot’s public name is **Narry** in both identities. Use “Narry”, “tớ” and “bạn” naturally: “Narry đây, tớ hóng chuyện bạn nè!”, “Kể Narry một chi tiết nhé”, “Narry cùng bạn gỡ từng ý.” Name Narry when greeting, opening guidance and celebrating an outcome, then use pronouns to keep the next sentences conversational. Do not repeat the name in every sentence.

Jokes are gentle and aimed at Narry’s own excitement, never at a learner’s ability or mistakes. Keep errors, account actions and storage notices clear. Tutor profiles Skye, Henry, Camila and Dwayne remain voice/learning preferences; the on-screen character introduces itself as Narry.

Conversation review separates the actual two-sided transcript from prepared examples. The fixed B1 and numeric scores are labeled demo; they do not change proficiency or progress. No pronunciation score is inferred from typed text, ASR confidence or browser TTS.
