## Later visual and motivational additions

The app now uses Quicksand display text and Nunito body/control text, an optional skippable Buddy introduction, Light/Dark/System appearance, local XP and daily quests, demo leagues/friends/feed, progress-derived achievements, a saved custom avatar, genuine QR/copy/device-share controls and Plus/Family plan previews. Fictional social content is labeled. Following and cheers do not contact real people or publish externally. Plan interest stays local; no payment, subscription or email notification is created.

## QA boundary

The current integrated TypeScript check and Vite production build pass. The final browser regression is recorded in [qa-2026-11-16.md](qa-2026-11-16.md), including responsive layout, lesson completion, settings persistence, local community/XP, avatar, QR/copy and light/dark appearance. These are observed prototype checks, not stakeholder acceptance of every row above. Screenshots with the `narra-final-` prefix reflect this pass; older captures refer to earlier layouts.

Device retesting should cover mic permission grant/deny and late responses, speech recognition availability, actual speech capture, automatic voice playback, local recording disposal on close/navigation, keyboard focus, four-tab navigation, EN/VI text lengths, light/dark/system appearance, reload persistence, QR scan destination, clipboard fallback and user-invoked native sharing. No real microphone hardware run, pronunciation-quality validation, real social-user test or payment test is claimed by this trace.

Authentication, live AI tutoring, academic pronunciation scoring, cloud identity/social data and scheduled notifications require connected services and their own validation. Local UAT notes do not mark those integrations complete.
